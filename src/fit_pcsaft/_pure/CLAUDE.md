# Surface tension and pareto fitting (`src/fit_pcsaft/_pure/`)

Implements Rehner & Gross (*J. Chem. Eng. Data* 2020, 65, 5698–5707): surface tension as a second, independent objective, to break the parameter degeneracy that vapour-pressure-plus-density fits leave behind for associating fluids.

- **`surface_tension.py`**: `predict_surface_tension(functional, T_vals, units, options)` — γ for a pure component from feos DFT, pDGT-initialized. Returns NaN wherever the VLE or interface solve fails; never raises. `SurfaceTensionOptions(n_grid=256)` is already grid-converged (~5 ms/point; `n_grid=2048` gives the same six digits at 8× the cost).
  - feos hardcodes `psi_dft = 1.3862` and its Python `PlanarInterface.from_pdgt` discards the bare pDGT surface tension, so results track the paper's **AAD_DFT** column, not AAD_pDGT.
  - Catches `BaseException`: feos signals unsupported input with `pyo3_runtime.PanicException`, which is not an `Exception`.
- **`pareto.py`**: `fit_pure_pareto(...)` → `ParetoResult`, solved with pymoo MOEA/D, in its ParallelMOEAD variant (derivative-free — large regions of parameter space have no VLE at all; plain MOEAD is loopwise and would feed the worker pool one point at a time). The `objectives` tuple picks the mode — see the next bullet.
  - **Five `objectives` tuples, the keys of `_DEFAULT_REFS`: two pairs at `n_obj=2`, the `("psat", "rho", "cp")` triple at `n_obj=3` (its own bullet below), and two that put γ on its own axis beside the separated bulk AARDs (end of this bullet).** `("vle", "sft")` (default) is Rehner & Gross 2020: `AAD_vle = mean(AARD(psat), AARD(rho))` in %, and `AAD_sft = mean|Δγ|` in mN/m — γ is an **absolute** deviation because it goes to zero at the critical point, where a relative one diverges. `("psat", "rho")` is Forte et al. 2018: the same two bulk AARDs, each on its own axis instead of pooled behind eq 30, with surface tension out of the objectives and (`sft_path` permitting) no DFT solve anywhere in the search itself — dropping `"sft"` from `objectives` also drops it from the graded-violation term (see the graded-violation bullet below), so a parameter set with no stable interface is not penalized under this pair. `sft_path` stays optional and, if passed, is still loaded and still reported — see the `hvap_path` bullet below for the one place it does DFT-solve, once, in `.select()`. Unsupported tuples, and any tuple containing `"sft"` without `sft_path`, raise `ValueError` before fitting starts. Under that pair the search is **batched**: `_evaluate_population` hands the whole population to feos as one `feos.Property.*_derivatives` call per property (`parameter_names=[]`, one AD row per candidate per temperature, rayon-parallel in Rust) and reproduces `_evaluate_point`'s `(f1, f2, violation)` row for row (`test_evaluate_population_matches_evaluate_point_row_for_row`). `("psat", "rho", "sft")` (`n_obj=3`) is the Forte pair plus `AAD_sft`, the same absolute mN/m as eq 31, so the front shows what surface tension costs psat and rho *individually* — which `("vle", "sft")` averages away; `("psat", "rho", "cp", "sft")` (`n_obj=4`) adds the cp axis, with the same `cp_path`/`cp_ig` requirement. Both contain `"sft"`, so both DFT-solve every evaluation on the per-point path and its process pool, and fold γ into the graded violation. Pinned by `test_psat_rho_sft_is_a_supported_triple` and `test_ref_dirs_generalise_to_four_objectives`; neither runs end-to-end in the suite, neither has been timed, and `.plot()` has no four-axis branch — on the quadruple it raises `ValueError`.
  - **Two MOEA/D settings differ from pymoo's stock `ParallelMOEAD`. Everything else is pymoo's.**

    | setting | pymoo 0.6.2 | here |
    |---|---|---|
    | crossover | `SBX(prob=1.0, eta=20)` | **`DEX(F=0.5, CR=1.0)`** — DE/rand/1/bin |
    | replacement cap `n_r` | uncapped | **2** (Li & Zhang 2009) |

    Unchanged, and pinned by `test_moead_settings_that_still_match_pymoo_stock`: `n_neighbors=20`, `prob_neighbor_mating=0.9`, `Tchebicheff`, `PM(eta=20)`. The two go together: `n_r=2` is MOEA/D-DE's cap and was adopted while pymoo's SBX stayed — half the algorithm. `variant="de"` completes it.

    **Measured on five problems** (`benchmarks/*-transfer/`, `benchmarks/de-confirm/`), budget ladder × 3 seeds, scored on a shared reference front. DE wins on water, carvone, `carvone-rho6` and thymol; camphor ties. On **thymol** — the only one TESIS fits this way — at 28 800 evaluations the hv bands are disjoint (de 22.94–23.94 vs sbx 19.87–22.23), pooled coverage runs de→sbx **0.995** against sbx→de **0.020**, and eq 32 reaches **0.5096–0.5349 on all three DE seeds** against the shipped 96 000-evaluation SBX fit's **0.5610**. SBX's tail is the other half of the case: one seed returned nothing under `AAD_vle` 5%, another 7 points at igd+ 4.86. Wall time is a wash (607–644 s vs 703–707 s).

    Two explanations were raised for camphor's tie and **both were falsified** — "DE needs a complicated Pareto set" by carvone (non-associating, 3-parameter, DE wins) and "camphor's ρ axis is starved at 6 points" by `carvone-rho6` (carvone thinned to 6 density points; DE still wins).

    **Three caveats.** (1) Below ~10 000 evaluations DE is **not** established — at 2 700 the metrics disagree on every compound tested. (2) `de_cr` is *not* resolved by measurement (best-cell gap 0.24 < seed spread 0.38); `1.0` is Li & Zhang's value, not ours. `de_f=0.5` is measured. (3) DE routes every default run through the vendored `_SeededDEX.do`, which fixes a pymoo bug — a pymoo upgrade is load-bearing, see `test_dex_do_pinned_to_vendored_pymoo_version`.

  - Infeasible sets: graded violation, folded into the objectives by `_penalize` (not a pymoo constraint); `_front_from` does the feasibility filtering. Full argument in `_penalize`'s docstring.
  - `_capped_replacement` (Li & Zhang's `nr`, pagmo's `limit=2`): pymoo's `MOEAD._replace` has no cap. Measured extents and the random-selection requirement are in its docstring and in `fit_pure_pareto`'s.
  - Table 1 comparison: the parameters, the factor-of-six/two mismatch and the data-range caveat are in `fit_pure_pareto`'s "Do not lean on it" paragraph.
  - **Scaling ranking, all with the cap, same budget** (`points | AAD_sft range | max gap | eq 32`): observed spans `131 | 0.64–1.36 | 0.225 | 2.961`; eq-32 refs `105 | 1.28–2.22 | 0.278 | 3.082`; raw `71 | 1.68–2.71 | 0.532 | 4.988`. Raw is worst by a wide margin. Single stochastic samples — 0.1 in eq 32 is not signal.
  - `n_jobs` applies wherever `_batched` is false: every tuple containing `"sft"`, and any tuple when `q != 0`. The bulk pair, and the cp triple, with `q == 0` never spawn a pool: `_evaluate_population` runs in-process on feos's own rayon threads, so `n_jobs` is a no-op there and the thread knob is `FEOS_MAX_THREADS` (or `feos.set_num_threads` before the first feos call). Where it applies, `n_jobs=-1` (default) evaluates the population across worker processes — every core bar two. **Processes, not threads: feos does not release the GIL** (measured 0.89x on a 16-thread pool, i.e. slower than serial). `feos.Identifier` is also unpicklable, so pymoo's `StarmapParallelization` cannot be used — the problem is a vectorized `Problem` that maps the population itself, and workers rebuild their own feos objects in an initializer. The pool uses **spawn**, since forking a process with feos' live rayon threads risks a child deadlock, and calls `feos.set_num_threads(1)` per worker to avoid oversubscription. Measured 4.3x on 14 workers with identical results.
  - The front is re-evaluated once after the search (at most `pop_size` points, under 2% of a real budget): `res.F` comes back in scaled, penalized space and a penalized row's true objectives cannot be recovered from it.
  - `quiet_solver=True` (default) silences fd-level stderr during the search; feos panics from Rust worker threads on infeasible parameter sets and Python cannot intercept those writes.
- **Validated** in `tests/test_pareto.py` against Tables 1 and 2 of the paper: water 2B/3B/4C reproduce AAD_DFT = 1.62/1.16/1.84 mN/m against the published 1.59/1.14/1.81, and the scheme ranking (4C best bulk, 3B best interface) holds.
